About FraudShield

FraudShield is an Intelligent Banking Fraud Detection and Transaction Monitoring System.

Our project monitors simulated banking transactions and identifies unusual activities using predefined fraud detection rules. When unusual activity is detected, the system generates an alert for review.

🎯 Our Mission

To identify unusual transactions and generate alerts for fraud analyst review.

🌍 Our Vision

To create awareness about banking security through intelligent transaction monitoring.

🚀 Our Goal

To provide a simple and user-friendly fraud monitoring application.

Bank Smart. Stay Secure.

Follow these important banking security tips to protect your personal information.

🔐 Protect Your Password

Never share your banking password with anyone.

📱 Never Share OTP

Never share your OTP or PIN with unknown people.

🔔 Check Alerts

Always review unexpected banking activity and security notifications.
